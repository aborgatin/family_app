Call the Skill tool twice, for "grilling" and "domain-modeling".

The design tree always has an **Announcement** branch (GLOSSARY): the push that tells every Member what this work gives them. Put it in the round after the feature's screens settle, with your recommended Russian `title`, `body` and `url` (the screen a tap opens), within the limits in `docs/architecture/deployment.md` "Adding an Announcement to a release". When no Member sees anything new (a fix, tooling, internal work), recommend **none** and say why. The branch is settled when the user accepts the text, or accepts none.
